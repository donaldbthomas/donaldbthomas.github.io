About

I'm a technical accounting leader based in San Francisco, with experience spanning standard-setting, Big Four advisory, and corporate finance.

I began at the FASB as a project manager on FIN 48, the interpretation on accounting for uncertainty in income taxes. Since then I've led corporate accounting and reporting for a multinational through a US GAAP to IFRS transition, advised clients as a Director at KPMG, and run financial accounting as a Senior Director at Qcells.

My focus is revenue recognition, business combinations, leasing and consolidations. I help companies reach the right answer, document it well, and build the controls to keep it right.
